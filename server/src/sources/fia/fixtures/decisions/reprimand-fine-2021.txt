The Stewards, having received a report from the Race Director, summoned (documents 27 & 28)
and heard from the drivers and team representatives, have considered the following matter and
determine the following:
No / Driver 31 - Esteban Ocon
Competitor Alpine F1 Team
Time 17:08
Session Qualifying
Fact Impeding of Car 5 by Car 31 in Turn 16.
Offence Breach of Article 31.5 of the FIA Formula One Sporting Regulations.
Decision Reprimand for the driver
(This is the driver’s 2nd Reprimand of the Season, 1st Driving Reprimand)
A fine of €10,000 is imposed on the Competitor (Alpine F1 Team)
Reason The Stewards heard from the driver of Car 31 (Esteban Ocon), the driver of Car 5
(Sebastian Vettel) and team representatives, and examined video, team radio and
telemetry evidence.
